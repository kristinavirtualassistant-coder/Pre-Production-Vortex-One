Acceptable use policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to everyone who uses Vortex One. It supports our Terms of service. Breaking it can lead to the actions described at the end.

You may not use Vortex One to

  • Break any law or regulation, including telemarketing, consent, do-not-call, call-recording, privacy and anti-discrimination laws.
  • Contact anyone who asked not to be contacted, or bypass or ignore do-not-call flags and suppression lists.
  • Impersonate a person or organization, hide that a caller is an AI, or use misleading or spoofed caller identification.
  • Threaten, harass, pressure or deceive the people you contact, or make false claims about a property, an offer or an outcome.
  • Select or exclude people in a way that discriminates on a protected characteristic, including under fair housing law.
  • Upload personal information you have no right to use, or data obtained unlawfully.
  • Collect sensitive data such as government ID numbers, financial account numbers or health information through the AI caller.
  • Run scams, fraud or schemes that target owners in financial or legal distress.

Protecting the service

  • Do not probe, scan or test the service for weaknesses without our written permission, or attempt to access another customer's workspace.
  • Do not scrape, bulk-export or resell Vortex One data beyond what your plan allows.
  • Do not bypass plan limits, metering or usage controls, or share credentials in a way that avoids seat limits.
  • Do not upload malware or interfere with the operation of the service.
  • Do not use the service or its outputs to build a competing product or train a competing model.

Using AI responsibly

Do not instruct the AI caller to give legal, financial, tax or property-management advice, to negotiate, to guarantee outcomes, or to claim it is human. Review AI outputs before relying on them.

Reporting and enforcement

Report abuse to info@remoteaccountmanagers.online. If we find or reasonably suspect a violation, we may remove content, pause campaigns, limit features, suspend or terminate accounts, and cooperate with authorities. Where reasonable we will warn you first, but we may act immediately to protect people or the service.
