Call recording notice

Last updated October 7, 2026

If you received a call

You may have received a call placed with Vortex One, a platform operated by The Remote Account Managers. The call was made on behalf of one of our customers, usually a real estate business. The caller is an AI assistant and says so at the start of the call.

Calls may be recorded

Calls placed with Vortex One may be recorded and transcribed. Where the law requires it, the caller tells you at the start of the call that it may be recorded. Some places require the consent of everyone on the call, and we aim to follow the strictest rule that applies.

Why we record

  • To keep an accurate record of what was said, including any request to stop calling.
  • To produce a transcript and an AI-generated summary for the customer's team.
  • To check quality, resolve complaints and show that calls followed the rules.
  • To meet legal and compliance obligations.

What is captured

A recording captures the audio of the call, and the transcript captures the words. We also record the number called, the time, the length and the outcome. We ask the AI caller not to collect sensitive information such as government ID numbers, financial account numbers or health information.

Who can access recordings

Recordings and transcripts are available to the customer who placed the call and to a small number of authorized people at The Remote Account Managers for support and compliance. Service providers that host, transcribe or analyze calls for us process them under contract. We do not sell recordings.

How long we keep them

Each customer sets how long recordings and transcripts are kept in their workspace. We also keep records longer where the law or a dispute requires it. When the period ends, they are deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

At any point you can:

  • Tell the caller you do not want to be recorded. The recording stops where we can, or the call ends.
  • Ask the caller to stop calling you. We honor do-not-call requests immediately.
  • Ask for a person instead of the AI caller.
  • Email us to ask for a copy of a recording or transcript about you, or to have it deleted. We may need to verify your identity, and we may need to keep some records for legal reasons.

Customer responsibilities

Each customer is responsible for configuring recording in line with the laws that apply to its calls, including recording consent and notice rules where it and the person called are located. See our Communications and do-not-call policy.

More information

How we handle personal information is explained in our Privacy policy. How the AI caller behaves is explained in our AI disclosure.
