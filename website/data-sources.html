Data sources and enrichment

Last updated October 7, 2026

What this page explains

Vortex One shows property and owner information and can enrich records with contact details. This page explains the kinds of data involved, where it comes from, how reliable it is and what you can do if something is wrong. It is a plain-language companion to our Privacy policy.

The kinds of data in Vortex One

  • Property data: address, parcel number, property type, size, assessed value, sales history and similar public-record attributes.
  • Ownership data: owner name, owner type (individual or entity), mailing address and length of ownership.
  • Property signals: indicators such as estimated equity, absentee status or recorded legal filings, used to help rank opportunities.
  • Contact data: phone numbers and email addresses for owners, returned when a user runs enrichment.
  • Customer-supplied data: records and notes that customers upload or create in their own workspace.

Where the data comes from

We use a combination of these sources:

  • Public records, such as county assessor, recorder and court records.
  • Licensed third-party data providers that compile property, ownership and contact information.
  • Data our customers provide in their own workspace.
  • Information generated in Vortex One itself, such as call outcomes, scores and summaries.

Third-party providers

We choose providers for the quality and lawfulness of their data and we may change providers over time. We list the service providers that process data for us in our Privacy policy, and we will publish a sub-processor list for business customers.

How enrichment works

When a user runs owner enrichment, Vortex One sends the property or owner details to one or more data providers and adds the results to the record. Enrichment uses credits on paid plans. Results can be partial, and a record may come back with no contact details at all.

Accuracy and freshness

Property and owner data can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Public records lag behind real events, people change numbers and addresses, and different sources can disagree. We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or timeliness of any data. Scores and signals are estimates, not facts about a person. Always verify important details before relying on them.

Correcting or removing data

If you see information about you that is wrong, or you want it removed:

  • Email info@remoteaccountmanagers.online with your name, the address or number concerned and what you want changed.
  • We will correct or suppress the information in Vortex One where we can verify the request, and we will pass the request to the relevant data provider or customer where required.
  • Do-not-call requests are honored immediately across the workspace. See our Communications and do-not-call policy.

Your rights and how we handle requests

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to correct it and to have it deleted. We handle these requests as described in our Privacy policy. Public-record information may remain at its original source even after we remove it from Vortex One.

Responsible use of data

  • Customers are responsible for using data lawfully, including telemarketing, consent, privacy and anti-discrimination rules.
  • Data must not be used to discriminate, to harass, or to target people in distress with deceptive offers. See our Acceptable use policy.
  • Contact details in read-only views are masked to limit unnecessary exposure.
  • Data must not be resold, scraped or exported beyond what a customer's plan allows.

Not a credit or eligibility report

Vortex One is not a consumer reporting agency. Its data and scores must not be used to decide a person's eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, housing or any other purpose covered by consumer reporting laws.
