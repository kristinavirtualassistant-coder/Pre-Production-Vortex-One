Communications and do-not-call policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Purpose

Vortex One helps customers contact property owners by phone and other channels. This policy explains how Vortex One supports respectful, lawful outreach and what we require of customers. It is not legal advice.

Respecting do-not-call requests

Vortex One is built to protect people who do not want to be contacted:

  • A request to stop being called, made on a call or by message, is honored immediately.
  • The contact is marked do-not-call and the flag is shown everywhere the contact appears in the workspace.
  • Do-not-call contacts are excluded from dialing and campaigns.
  • Customers can import their own internal do-not-call lists for matching before outreach.

Customer responsibilities

Each customer is responsible for the lawfulness of its own outreach. Depending on where you and the people you contact are located, the law that applies can include:

  • The Telephone Consumer Protection Act and the Telemarketing Sales Rule, including National Do Not Call Registry rules and your own internal do-not-call list.
  • Consent requirements for automated or artificial-voice calls and for text messages. AI-generated voices can count as artificial voices under U.S. rules, so confirm your consent position before using the AI caller.
  • Calling-time limits, which in the U.S. generally run from 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. in the recipient's local time, plus any stricter state rules.
  • State and local telemarketing registration, caller identification and call-recording consent laws.
  • Anti-discrimination and fair housing laws when selecting who to contact.

Calls made through Vortex One

  • AI calls disclose that the caller is an AI and name the business calling.
  • Callers use accurate caller identification and may not spoof numbers.
  • The AI caller stops when asked and does not pressure, mislead or negotiate.
  • Recording is configured by the customer and disclosed where the law requires.

Registry and list checks

Customers are responsible for scrubbing their lists against the registries and rules that apply to them before launching a campaign. Matching tools in Vortex One help, but they do not replace the customer's own compliance obligations.

How to ask not to be contacted

If you receive a call or message from a Vortex One customer and do not want more, tell the caller, reply STOP to any text, or email info@remoteaccountmanagers.online with your phone number and we will make sure it is suppressed and forward your request to the customer.

Complaints and enforcement

We investigate complaints about calls placed with Vortex One. We may pause campaigns, limit features or suspend accounts that breach this policy, our Acceptable use policy or the law. Contact info@remoteaccountmanagers.online to report a concern.
