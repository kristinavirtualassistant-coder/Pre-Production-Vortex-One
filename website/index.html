Features

Everything a real-estate operations team runs on

One workspace for the full cycle: find the property, reach the owner, follow up on time, and see what's working.

CRM and pipeline

Leads, contacts, notes, tasks and a pipeline board, with every call and message on the record.

Dialer

Manual and batch dialing with dispositions, call timers and transcripts. Do-not-call requests are honored everywhere the contact appears.

Campaigns

Email, SMS and call campaigns with eligibility rules, scheduling and engagement tracking.

Property and owner intelligence

Search properties and public records, enrich owner details, and score leads so callers start with the warmest ones.

Workflows

Automate follow-ups, assignments and data syncs with versioned workflows, schedules, retries and run logs.

AI agents

Agents that summarize calls, draft follow-ups and help qualify leads. AI output is labeled as AI, and the AI caller always says it is an AI.

How it works

Up and running in an afternoon

Create your workspace

Sign up, verify your email and invite your team. Each company gets its own private workspace.

Bring in your leads

Work the queue

Dial, follow up and automate the rest. Analytics show which campaigns and callers convert.

Security

Built for teams that handle owner data

Your workspace is yours alone. Access is decided on the server, never in the browser.

  • Each company's data is isolated at the database level.
  • Roles for admins, managers, agents and members control who can see and change what.
  • Multi-factor authentication, session management and sign-in protection.
  • Activity is audit-logged.
  • Do-not-call requests are honored immediately.
