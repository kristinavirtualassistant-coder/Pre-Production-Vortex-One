How it works

From list to booked conversation in four steps

1

Import

Bring in your owner lists. Do-not-call records are matched before anything is dialed.

2

Score

3

Call

4

Follow up

Built to be honest

An AI that says it is an AI

Vortex One talks to owners like a sharp operations lead: calm, specific, brief. It discloses that it is an AI, honors do-not-call requests the moment they are made, and never gives legal, financial or tax advice, negotiates or guarantees an outcome.
