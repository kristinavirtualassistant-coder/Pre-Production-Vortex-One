Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Summary

You can cancel Vortex One at any time. Cancellation stops future charges and your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. New paid subscribers can ask for a refund within 14 days of their first payment. Usage that has already been consumed is not refundable.

Free plan

The Free plan has no charge. You can stop using it or close your account at any time.

How to cancel

You can cancel in either of these ways:

  • In your workspace, open billing settings and choose Cancel subscription.
  • Email info@remoteaccountmanagers.online from the address on the account and ask us to cancel.

What happens when you cancel

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep full access until then.
  • We do not charge you again after cancellation, and renewal reminders stop.
  • After the period ends, the account moves to read-only access for 30 days so you can export your data. After that, we delete workspace data as described in our Privacy policy, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Active campaigns and AI calling stop when the paid period ends.

Refunds on monthly plans

Monthly fees are not refunded for a partial month after the first 14 days. If you are a first-time paid subscriber, you can request a refund of your first monthly payment within 14 days of paying it.

Refunds on annual plans

If you are a first-time paid subscriber on an annual plan, you can request a full refund within 14 days of paying. After that, annual fees are not refunded for the remaining months, though your plan stays active until the end of the year you paid for.

Usage, credits and metered charges

  • AI calling minutes, owner enrichment credits and property searches that have been used are not refundable, because the provider cost has already been incurred.
  • Unused prepaid credits are refundable only within the 14-day first-purchase window. Otherwise they stay available until they expire, as stated when you bought them, and they have no cash value.
  • If a feature fails and consumes credits or minutes without delivering a result, contact us and we will restore the credits or refund the charge.

Billing errors and duplicate charges

If we charge you incorrectly, for example twice for the same period or at the wrong price, email us within 60 days and we will correct it and refund the difference.

Plan changes

  • Upgrades take effect immediately. We charge the difference for the rest of the current period.
  • Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. We do not refund the price difference for the current period.
  • If a downgrade brings you under your current usage or seat count, you may need to remove users or data before it applies.

Cancellation by us

If we cancel or suspend your account because of a breach of our Terms or Acceptable use policy, we do not refund fees already paid. If we end a paid service for reasons unrelated to you, we will refund the unused part of the period you paid for.

Price changes and renewals

Paid plans renew automatically until cancelled. We will tell you before a price increase takes effect, and you can cancel before the new price applies.

How we pay refunds

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through our payment processor. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Your legal rights

This policy does not limit rights you may have under consumer protection law where you live. Those rights apply in addition to what is written here.

Contact

To cancel or request a refund, email info@remoteaccountmanagers.online with the account email and the date of the charge. The Remote Account Managers.
