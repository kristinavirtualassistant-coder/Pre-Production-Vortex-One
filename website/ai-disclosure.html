AI disclosure

Last updated October 7, 2026

Where Vortex One uses AI

Vortex One uses artificial intelligence in these places:

  • AI caller: places first-touch outbound calls on behalf of a customer and records the outcome.
  • Lead scoring: ranks prospects and shows the reasons behind each score.
  • Summaries: writes call summaries and suggested follow-up tasks.

The AI caller says it is an AI

The AI caller identifies itself as an AI assistant in its opening statement and on request. It does not claim or imply that it is a human, and it states the business it is calling for.

What the AI caller will not do

The AI caller is limited to opening a conversation and capturing the owner's response. It does not:

  • Give legal, financial, tax, real estate or property-management advice.
  • Negotiate price or terms, or make offers on a customer's behalf.
  • Guarantee outcomes, returns or timelines.
  • Continue a call after the person asks to stop or to be removed from the list.

Handing off to people

When an owner is interested, asks for a person or raises something outside the AI caller's limits, the lead is handed to a human on the customer's team with the call context. Anyone on a call can ask to speak with a person.

Do-not-call and removal requests

A request to stop being called is honored immediately and the contact is marked do-not-call across the workspace. See our Communications and do-not-call policy.

Recording and transcripts

Calls may be recorded and transcribed to provide the service, and callers are told when a call may be recorded where the law requires it. Customers are responsible for configuring recording in line with the laws that apply to them.

Accuracy and human review

Scores, transcripts and summaries are AI-generated and can contain errors. They are labeled as AI-generated in the product. Customers should review them before acting, and no decision about a person should rely on them alone.

Questions or concerns

If you received a call from a Vortex One AI caller and have a question or complaint, email info@remoteaccountmanagers.online.
