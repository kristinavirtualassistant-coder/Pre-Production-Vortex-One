Privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Who we are

Vortex One is a property intelligence, owner enrichment, CRM and AI calling platform operated by The Remote Account Managers ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and the choices you have.

There are two groups of people this policy covers: customers who create a Vortex One account, and the property owners and other contacts whose information our customers load into their workspace.

Information we collect

We collect the following, depending on how you use Vortex One:

  • Account information: your name, work email, company, role and login credentials.
  • Workspace data you add or generate: lead lists, property and owner records, notes, tags, pipeline stages, tasks and campaign settings.
  • Call data: dialed numbers, call outcomes, AI call transcripts, summaries and, where enabled and lawful, recordings.
  • Enrichment data: owner contact details and property attributes we or our data providers return when you run enrichment.
  • Billing information: handled by our payment processor. We receive plan, status and the last digits of a card, not the full card number.
  • Usage and device data: pages viewed, features used, log data, IP address, browser type and cookies needed to run and secure the service.
  • Messages you send us, such as support requests and demo requests.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain and secure Vortex One, including scoring, enrichment, dialing, CRM and automation features.
  • Process payments, apply plan limits and meter usage such as AI calling and enrichment credits.
  • Honor do-not-call and removal requests and keep suppression lists current.
  • Provide support, send service notices and respond to requests.
  • Improve reliability and features, and detect fraud and abuse.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Customer data and property owner data

Customers decide which owner and contact records to load into their workspace and who to contact. For that data we act on the customer's instructions, and each customer is responsible for having a lawful basis to hold and contact those people.

If you are a property owner or contact and want your information removed, or you no longer want to be called, tell the caller, reply to any message, or email us at info@remoteaccountmanagers.online. We will honor do-not-call requests promptly and pass access and deletion requests to the relevant customer where required.

AI processing

Vortex One uses AI models to score leads, summarize calls and hold first-touch calls. Calls disclose that the caller is an AI. AI outputs can be wrong, so customers should review them before relying on them. See our AI disclosure for details.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the service:

  • Service providers acting for us: cloud hosting, telephony, AI model providers, data enrichment providers, payment processing, email and analytics.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and the integrity of the service.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition or asset sale, with notice where required.
  • With your direction, for example when you connect another tool.

Retention

We keep account and workspace data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, accounting and security purposes. Call recordings and transcripts are kept for the period configured for the workspace. You can ask us to delete your workspace data, subject to legal retention requirements.

Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit and other safeguards appropriate to the data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us promptly about any suspected unauthorized access.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to opt out of marketing. To exercise a right, email info@remoteaccountmanagers.online. We may need to verify your identity first.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies that are needed to sign you in, remember preferences and keep the service secure. You can control cookies in your browser, though some features may not work without them.

Children

Vortex One is a business tool and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

International use

We may process and store information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for material changes, notify account holders.
