Cookie policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, do the same kind of job. This policy explains how Vortex One, operated by The Remote Account Managers, uses them on our website and application.

Cookies we use

We group them by purpose:

  • Strictly necessary: keep you signed in, protect your session, prevent fraud and abuse, and remember choices needed to run the service, such as your cookie preference. These cannot be switched off in our systems.
  • Preferences: remember settings such as theme or the view you last used, so you do not have to set them again.
  • Analytics: help us understand which pages and features are used so we can improve the service. These are used only if you allow them where the law requires consent.

What we do not use

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell data collected through cookies.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by service providers that help us run the site, such as analytics and payment providers. These providers can see limited information about your visit and handle it under their own policies. We list providers that process data for us in our Privacy policy.

How long cookies last

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies stay for a set period, usually from a few days to 12 months, or until you delete them.

Your choices

  • Cookie banner: where required, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies when you first visit and change your choice at any time from the cookie settings link in the footer.
  • Browser settings: you can block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop sign-in and other features from working.
  • Opt-out signals: where the law requires it, we honor browser signals that tell us you do not want to be tracked.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as we add or remove cookies. We will change the date above and, for material changes, ask for your choice again where required.
